Tap around

The three tabs work. Tapping any pot opens its detail. The timeframe pills change every number on the screen. The "Synced 4 min ago" chip and the Appearance switch in Setup flip light/dark.

The red dot on Rules is the over-cap warning; it's the only red chrome in the app, and only Side Bet can cause it.

Rules the design follows
Pounds before percent. Every percentage is followed by what it means in money. "Up 6%" alone is banned.
One number is the hero. Everything you own, once, at the top, bigger than anything else on the screen.
Silence is a result. "Nothing needs you" is the most common state and it's designed for, not an empty screen.
Side Bet looks different. Hatched fence, hard border, its own hot colour. You can't mistake it for the safe money.
No chart without a sentence. Charts always carry a plain-English caption underneath.
Read-only, said out loud. Every screen ends with a line reminding you Pip can't trade.

07 — Desktop & tablet
Two breakpoints

≥ 768 (tablet) — the tab bar leaves the bottom and becomes a 76px icon rail down the left. Content stays one column, capped at 640, with the ground either side. Nothing gains a column; the phone screen simply stops being stretched.

≥ 1120 (desktop) — the rail expands to a 232px sidebar with labels, and content goes to a 1080 max-width two- or three-track grid.

Sidebar, not top nav

A top nav would put a horizontal band above the hero number and cost it its place as the first thing you see. The sidebar keeps the top-left of the content area free, so every screen still opens on one big number.

It carries the same three destinations, the same red dot on Rules, and picks up two things the phone hides in chrome: who you're signed in as, and the read-only badge.

What the width buys

More at once, but only where scrolling was the tax: the three pots sit side by side, the chart sits beside the holdings, the feed sits beside the shape. Nothing new is invented for desktop — the same blocks, re-laid.

Content stops at 1080. Either side is plain ground — no widgets, no ticker, no second column of filler. Empty ground is the point.

The fourth column: yes, on desktop only

The phone table stops at three columns because 390px is the limit, not because three is the right number. Desktop restores the column the phone had to fold into the subtitle: Holding · Value · Today · Since you bought, with the sparkline in its own gutter rather than squeezed between value and chevron.

That's the ceiling. No weight column, no cost basis, no day range — those belong at the broker. And every percentage still has its pounds next to it.

08 — The mark at icon sizes

The geometry holds down to 64. Below that the third seed falls under two device pixels and the gaps close up, so there is a second cut — same three seeds, same arrangement, same order of size. Nothing is dropped and nothing is rearranged: the radii compress toward each other and the gaps open.

Standard cut · 48px and up
r 13 / 10 / 6.5 — the mark as drawn everywhere else
180 · App Store
64 · holds
32 · third seed dissolves
16 · mush
Small cut · 16–48px
r 13.5 / 11.5 / 9 · centres pushed out 1.5–2 units

The verdict
Three things change and nothing else:
Size contrast compresses. 13 / 10 / 6.5 becomes 13.5 / 11.5 / 9 — the big seed still reads biggest, but the small one survives a 16px grid.
Gaps open. Centres move out by 1.5–2 units so the seeds don't fuse into a clover when antialiasing softens the edges.
Nothing is dropped. Three seeds is the product; two would be a different brand. At 16px they read as three warm dots, which is enough to be recognised as Pip after you've seen it at 180.
Ship both cuts as separate SVGs. Switch at 48px. The one-ink favicon uses the small cut's geometry.
